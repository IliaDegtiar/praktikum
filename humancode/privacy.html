Privacy Policy

Human Code Studio · last updated October 23, 2026

Human Code Studio is a private, single-user tool that the owner of the channel “Read The Human Code” (Ilia Degtiarev) runs on his own computer to upload his own short videos to his own social media accounts. It has no other users and offers no public sign-up.

What data the tool accesses

  • When the owner connects his own YouTube channel, Google gives the tool an access token for the permissions the owner approves: upload videos (youtube.upload), view his own channel and videos (youtube.readonly) and view YouTube Analytics reports for his own channel (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • The tool uses this access only to upload the owner's own videos with their titles and descriptions, to confirm the uploads, and to read view counts, likes, comments counts and average view percentage of his own videos so that he can see how they perform.
  • When the owner connects his own TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic account information (account ID and display name), within the permissions the owner approves.
  • The tool uses this access only to upload the owner's own videos and their captions to his own account and to check that the uploads succeeded.

What data the tool does not collect

The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person. It does not read followers' data, messages or comments, does not track viewers, and contains no tracking or advertising code.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform they belong to (Google/YouTube or TikTok).

Google API Services

Human Code Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from YouTube APIs is not used for advertising, is not transferred to third parties and is not used to train AI models. The tool also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the tool's local files removes all stored tokens. Questions or deletion requests: thehumancode619@gmail.com.
